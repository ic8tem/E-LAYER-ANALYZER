## Colour schemes

The schemes are those of YA-MS, which in turn come from YAEC: **Light**, **Night**, **Black**, eight **pastels** (light blue, orange, aquamarine, green, pink, lilac, indigo, brown) and **Meteor trail**. They are chosen at the top right.

## Languages (28)

Italiano, English, Deutsch, Français, Español, Português, Nederlands, Svenska, Norsk (bokmål), Dansk, Íslenska, Eesti, Latviešu, Lietuvių, Русский, Українська, Türkçe, Afrikaans, العربية, עברית, اردو, हिन्दी, বাংলা, Bahasa Indonesia, Tagalog, 中文 (简体), 日本語, 한국어.

The interface of E-LAYER ANALYZER and of YA-MS, the Technical document and the Sources are all translated. In the F.A.I./aurora view the term **Scatter trail** is the same in every language.
