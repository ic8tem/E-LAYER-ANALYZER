**E-LAYER ANALYZER** is a free program for radio amateurs that analyses every VHF propagation mode involving the ionosphere's **E layer**: **sporadic-E** (including intercontinental multi-hop), **F.A.I.** (field-aligned irregularities), **meteor scatter** (with the built-in YA-MS program) and **radio aurora**. It runs on **Windows (64 and 32 bit), Linux, Raspberry Pi and Mac**, in **28 languages**, and writes nothing outside its own folder.

## The tabs

| Tab | What it does |
|---|---|
| [SPORADIC-E](Sporadic-E) | Es spots on the map, first hop of both stations, Es clouds on Maidenhead squares, MUF for your station |
| [BENCHMARK Es](Benchmark-Es) | Test bench on a whole ADIF log (even from another station): QSOs per day and per hour, skip by sector, estimated foEs, multi-hop check, cloud drift |
| [BENCHMARK 50 MHz](Benchmark-50) | Multi-hop sporadic-E on 50 MHz from one or two ADIF logs: Mercator map, day and time, optional foEs, enlarged path with the WWL, QSOs within 150 km |
| [F.A.I.](FAI) | Scatter trail of the scatter points for a pair of stations, and isoflection loci to check a point |
| [METEOR SCATTER](Meteor-Scatter) | The complete YA-MS program (shower efficiency, radars, forecasts) |
| [AURORA](Aurora) | Auroral oval, NOAA Kp and solar wind, auroral scatter trail and isoflection, recommended beam heading |
| [DATA COLLECTION](Data-Collection) | Three telnet sessions (DX cluster, RBN, DM4X with PSKReporter), collected spots, CALL3 database |
| [LOG ADIF](Log-ADIF) | Squares worked and confirmed band by band from 28 MHz up, from your ADIF or from LoTW |
| [SOURCES, SUMMARY OF THE CALCULATION CHOICES, USER MANUAL, TECHNICAL REPORT](Sources-and-Technical-Document) | Sources, formulas and calculation choices, in-depth user manual, technical report: exportable to Word |
