# Frequently asked questions

[← Home](Home)

**The map is empty.** On first start a network connection is needed to download the coastlines (Natural Earth); after that they stay in `data/mappa`.

**No spots arrive.** In DATA COLLECTION, check that the callsign is entered (at the top or in the Login field) and that the session is connected; on ON0DXK enable VHF with `set/filter dxbm/pass 6`.

**"Calculation not possible" popup.** For the model there is no point that meets the reflection condition and is seen by both stations, or the chosen point is below the horizon. Try another height or turn on refraction.

**Sporadic-E on 432 MHz?** It does not apply: see [SPORADIC-E](Sporadic-E).

**On Mac the program will not open.** Use **Installa E-LAYER-ANALYZER.command** (right-click > Open): it removes the "downloaded from the Internet" block.
