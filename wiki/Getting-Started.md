# Getting started

[← Home](Home)

1. At the top, enter **Callsign**, **Locator** (6 characters, e.g. JN70CN) and **Height** in metres, then press **Apply**.
2. Choose the **language** and the **colour scheme** at the top right (the window is rebuilt immediately).
3. Go to **DATA COLLECTION > Telnet sessions**: check host, port and commands of the three sessions and press **Connect**. With **Connect at startup** they open by themselves.
4. After a few minutes the spots appear in **SPORADIC-E**, **F.A.I.** and **AURORA**.

## Maps

- Mouse **wheel**: zooms in around the cursor.
- **Drag** with the left button: moves the map.
- Top right: coordinates and locator of the point under the cursor.
- **Europe** / **World** buttons: starting views.
- Every page with a map gives 65 % to the map and 35 % to the data; the divider can be dragged.
